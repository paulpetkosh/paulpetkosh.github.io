SiteLock Camera

The job-site camera that stays the way you set it.

Privacy Policy

SiteLock Camera works like the iPhone camera with one big difference: you can lock the orientation in Settings, so every photo and video saves portrait or landscape no matter how the phone is tilted. It also keeps photos organized by job, stamps each one with the date, time, address, and GPS, and turns a job's photos into a PDF report.

Features

  • Orientation lock that only changes in Settings
  • Jobs with their own photo albums, notes, voice notes, and tags
  • Photo stamp with date, time, address, GPS, compass, and your logo
  • Markup: arrows, boxes, text, callouts, and dimension lines
  • Shot lists, before-and-after ghost overlay, and glove mode
  • LiDAR measuring on Pro iPhones
  • PDF photo reports, zip exports, and backups
  • Roofing add-on: pitch reader, hail test squares, and roofing shot lists

Support

Questions, problems, or ideas? Email info@blacklineroofing.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

How do I change the orientation lock? In the app, tap the gear, then Orientation. It's also in iPhone Settings > Apps > SiteLock.

How do I get my add-ons on a new iPhone? Open Settings in the app, then Add-Ons, and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought them with.

Where are my photos? In the job inside the app, and in the Photos app in an album named "SiteLock – job name".
