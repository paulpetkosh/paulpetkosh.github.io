Privacy Policy

SiteLock Camera

Effective October 30, 2026

In short: your photos, videos, jobs, notes, and recordings stay on your iPhone and in your own Photos library. SiteLock Camera has no account and no servers of its own, and it never uploads your work. The free version shows ads from Google, which collect some device data as described below. Buying Remove Ads or any add-on turns the ads off.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Photos and videos are saved in the app and in your Photos library. They aren't sent anywhere unless you share or export them yourself.
  • Jobs, notes, tags, markup, shot lists, and measurements are stored in the app on your iPhone. If iCloud Backup is on, they're included in your iPhone's backup to your own iCloud account, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Voice notes are recorded and typed out on the iPhone itself. The recordings and text never leave the device.
  • Camera, LiDAR, and motion sensors are used only to take photos and videos, measure, read roof pitch, and keep the orientation lock working.

Location

With your permission, SiteLock Camera uses your location to put the address, GPS coordinates, and compass direction on photos, to write GPS into the photo files, and to suggest the job you're at. To turn coordinates into a street address, the app uses Apple's address lookup, which sends the location to Apple under Apple's privacy policy. SiteLock Camera doesn't keep a history of where you've been, and doesn't send your location to anyone else. You can turn location off in iPhone Settings > Apps > SiteLock > Location.

Ads (free version only)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads never appear on the camera screen. To show and measure ads, Google's software may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking when the iPhone asks
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device type, iOS version, and app version
  • which ads were shown and tapped, and crash and performance data about the ads

If you don't allow tracking, you still see ads, but they aren't personalized from your activity in other apps. Where the law requires it (for example, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice later in the app's Settings. Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Buying Remove Ads or any add-on stops the ads, and Google's ad software no longer runs.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. SiteLock Camera never sees your payment details. It only learns from Apple which add-ons your Apple Account owns, so it can unlock them.

Sharing and exports

When you share a photo, send a PDF report, or export a zip or backup, it goes only where you send it, such as Messages, Mail, Files, or OneDrive. That service's own privacy policy then applies.

What SiteLock Camera does not do

  • It doesn't sell your information.
  • It doesn't use analytics or tracking of its own.
  • It doesn't collect your name, email, or contacts.

Children

SiteLock Camera is a work tool made for adults and isn't directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email the support address listed on the SiteLock Camera App Store page.
